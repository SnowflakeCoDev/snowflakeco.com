Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your Mama Care Hub account from inside the app. Pick your device, then follow the three steps below.

Path More→ Account Settings→ Delete Account
1

Open More

Tap More in the bottom menu, then select Account Settings.

2

Tap Delete Account

In Account Settings, scroll down and tap Delete Account.

3

Confirm

Tap Delete in the confirmation box to finish, or Cancel to stop.

What happens to your data

From this page, users can permanently delete all personal data associated with their account.

Subscription and order history are retained only in anonymized form for legal and financial compliance, and contain no personally identifiable information.
