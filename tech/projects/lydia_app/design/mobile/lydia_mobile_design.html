M14 — ProfileRTLحسابي

Design Tokens Applied

Brand red #C8102E drives primary CTAs and accents. Tajawal display + Cairo body fonts. 8-pt spacing. 12 px card radius. Uniform --shadow-sm on cards.

RTL-First Layout

Every screen here is rendered RTL. Cart steppers, product carousels, navigation arrows, and progress indicators all mirror correctly. LTR is achieved by flipping a single dir attribute.

Thumb-Zone Optimized

Bottom tab bar with 5 tabs. The center cart action is elevated (FAB-style) to catch the thumb. WhatsApp "Ask the Pharmacist" floats above the tab bar — present everywhere without consuming viewport.

States Demonstrated

  • Cart: stock-cap warning banner (EC-3)
  • Product: in-stock indicator + discount tag
  • Home: live "Open Now" badge (US-1.5)
  • Profile: language toggle (AR/EN)
  • Hero: paginated carousel with active dot
